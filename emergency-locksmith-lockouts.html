Locked out? Common situations we can help assess

Whether a door has shut behind you or the locking mechanism has failed, the right approach depends on the lock and the cause of the problem.

Locked out of the house

Door closed or locked with no way back in? Call to discuss entry options and availability. We aim to assess suitable non-destructive methods first where practical.

Keys left inside

If your keys are indoors and the door has shut, the lock type and whether it is deadlocked affect the available entry methods.

Lost or stolen house keys

We can discuss gaining entry where needed and whether a cylinder or lock change would be appropriate for security.

Key broken in the lock

A snapped key can prevent normal operation. Avoid pushing the broken piece further into the cylinder or forcing another key.

Key turns but the door won't open

The fault may be in the cylinder, latch, gearbox or multipoint locking mechanism. Read about jammed UPVC doors.

UPVC or composite door jammed shut

A failed gearbox or locking point can leave a door stuck even when you have the correct key. See UPVC door lock repairs.

What to do if you're locked out

Call 07470 236030 and explain where you are, the door type and what happened. If possible, tell us whether the key is lost, inside, broken or still turning. We can discuss availability, likely next steps and pricing before work is completed.

Do not force the door or lock, as this may cause avoidable damage. If there is an immediate threat to life or safety, contact the appropriate emergency services rather than waiting for a locksmith.

Frequently asked questions

Can you open a door without damaging the lock?

Non-destructive entry may be possible depending on the lock, how the door is secured and its condition. It cannot be guaranteed in every case.

Can you change the lock after a lockout?

Where suitable parts are available, a lock or cylinder can be replaced when needed, including after keys have been lost or stolen.

How quickly can you get here?

Attendance depends on your location and current availability. Call for an estimate rather than relying on a fixed response-time promise.
