COMMON LOCKING PROBLEMS

What has gone wrong with your door?

The same symptom does not always mean the same component has failed. Checking how the door behaves helps narrow down the cause before parts are replaced.

UPVC door won't lockHandle won't liftHandle feels floppyKey won't turnDoor jammed shutDoor needs liftingKey keeps spinningWindow won't open

Door locks that work when open but not when closed

If the mechanism operates normally with the door open but becomes stiff when the door is closed, alignment or the position of the keeps may be contributing to the problem. Repeatedly forcing the handle can place extra load on the gearbox and multipoint mechanism.

When the handle or key suddenly stops working

A sudden change can indicate a problem inside the gearbox, cylinder or multipoint mechanism. Correct identification is particularly important because UPVC door lock parts vary by manufacturer, dimensions and configuration.

CAMBRIDGE LOCKSMITH QUESTIONS

Useful things to know before arranging a repair

Does a faulty UPVC lock mean I need a new door?

Not necessarily. Many problems are caused by individual locking components or alignment. The door and frame may still be perfectly serviceable.

Can just the gearbox be replaced?

Sometimes. It depends on the mechanism, the fault and whether a suitable replacement component can be correctly identified.

Why is my UPVC door getting harder to lock?

Possible causes include alignment changes, pressure at the keeps or wear within the locking mechanism. Continuing to force it can make the problem worse.

Do you only work on UPVC locks?

No. UPVC and composite door and window locks are the speciality, but general residential locksmith work is also undertaken.
