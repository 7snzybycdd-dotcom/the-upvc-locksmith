Repairing UPVC door locking problems

A door that will not lock does not automatically need a complete new door. Problems can be caused by the multipoint mechanism, centre gearbox, euro cylinder, handles, keeps, hinges or door alignment. Correct diagnosis helps avoid replacing parts unnecessarily.

Door won't lock

If the handle will not lift or the locking points will not engage, the mechanism or alignment may need attention.

Key won't turn

A stiff or non-turning key can indicate a cylinder problem, pressure on the mechanism or an internal gearbox fault.

Door jammed shut

Failed locking mechanisms can leave a UPVC door stuck closed. The fault needs identifying before the correct repair is chosen.

Multipoint lock and gearbox faults

Many UPVC doors use a multipoint locking strip with hooks, rollers, mushrooms or bolts. The central gearbox operates these locking points. When a gearbox or another part of the mechanism fails, replacing the correct component can often restore the existing door.
