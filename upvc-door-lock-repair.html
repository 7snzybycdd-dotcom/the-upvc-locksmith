Common UPVC door problems and possible causes

You may search for “UPVC door handle won't lift”, “key turns but door won't open” or “UPVC door won't lock” without knowing which part is faulty. These symptoms can have several causes, and an inspection is needed to confirm the problem.

My UPVC door handle won't lift

A handle that will not lift can mean the multipoint locking points are binding against the keeps, the door has dropped or the centre gearbox is failing. If the handle lifts normally with the door open but not when closed, alignment is a possibility. Avoid forcing it.

The handle lifts but the key won't turn

The locking mechanism may not be reaching its fully engaged position, or the euro cylinder or gearbox may be faulty. The cause needs checking before replacing a cylinder.

My key turns but the door won't open

The cylinder may turn without releasing the latch or locking points if a gearbox or multipoint mechanism has failed. A jammed door needs careful diagnosis rather than repeated force.

My UPVC door won't lock unless I lift it

A dropped or misaligned door can put pressure on the hooks, rollers or bolts. Hinge adjustment, keep adjustment or worn hardware may be involved.

The UPVC door handle is floppy or loose

Loose fixings, a worn handle spring cassette or a failed gearbox spring can leave a handle drooping. A replacement handle is not always the solution.

The handle is stiff or difficult to push down

Binding locking points, a damaged gearbox or poor alignment may make the handle hard to operate. Forcing it can worsen an existing fault.

The door locks when open but not when closed

This can suggest that locking points are not lining up with the keeps, although wear in the mechanism may also contribute. An inspection can identify whether adjustment or replacement is needed.

The key is stuck in the door lock

A worn or damaged euro cylinder, an incorrectly positioned cam or pressure from the multipoint mechanism can prevent normal key removal. Avoid snapping the key by forcing it.

The UPVC door won't close properly

Dropped hinges, a misaligned sash, damaged keeps or a latch that is not engaging can prevent a door closing securely. The correct repair depends on the actual fault.

The door is jammed shut

A broken centre gearbox, failed hook or bolt mechanism, cylinder issue or severe misalignment may leave a door stuck closed. See our dedicated jammed UPVC door repair guidance. Read about jammed door repairs.

Does a faulty UPVC door need a whole new lock?

Not necessarily. Depending on the fault and parts availability, it may be possible to adjust hinges or keeps, replace a euro cylinder or handle, repair a centre gearbox or fit a compatible multipoint locking mechanism. A complete new door is often unnecessary when the door and frame are serviceable.

What should I do if my UPVC door won't lock?

Do not force the key or lift the handle with excessive pressure. If the door cannot be secured, arrange an inspection promptly. Photos of the handles, locking edge and mechanism faceplate can help identify the hardware before a visit.

Multipoint lock and gearbox faults

Many UPVC doors use a multipoint locking strip with hooks, rollers, mushrooms or bolts. The central gearbox operates these locking points. When a gearbox or another part of the mechanism fails, replacing the correct component can often restore the existing door.
