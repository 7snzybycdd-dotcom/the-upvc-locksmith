Common UPVC window problems and what may cause them

Searching for “UPVC window won't close” or “window handle turns but won't open”? You do not need to know the name of the broken part. These are common symptoms we can investigate. The cause can only be confirmed by inspecting the window.

My UPVC window won’t close

If the sash catches the frame or the handle will not move fully into the closed position, possible causes include dropped hinges, misaligned keeps or a locking mechanism that is not moving freely. Avoid forcing the handle, as this can cause further damage.

My window handle turns but the window won’t open

A loose or spinning handle can indicate a worn spindle, a damaged handle or a failed gearbox inside the espagnolette mechanism. The handle alone is not always the faulty part.

My UPVC window is stuck shut

The locking cams or shootbolts may remain engaged if the mechanism fails. The fault needs checking before deciding whether the gearbox or full locking strip requires replacement.

The window won’t lock

If the handle moves but the window will not secure, locking points may be missing their keeps, the sash may have dropped or the mechanism may be worn. A window that cannot be secured should be assessed promptly.

The window won’t shut at the top or bottom

A gap at one corner may point to hinge wear, sash alignment or an obstruction at the frame. It does not necessarily mean the entire window needs replacing.

My window handle is stiff or hard to turn

Resistance can result from misalignment, worn moving parts or locking points binding against their keeps. Forcing a stiff handle can break the gearbox.

The window handle spins freely

If the handle rotates without moving the locking points, the spindle or gearbox may no longer be engaging. Inspection is needed to distinguish a simple handle replacement from a mechanism repair.

The window closes but there is a draught

A window that looks closed may not be pulling evenly against its seals. Hinges, keeps, compression adjustment or worn seals may contribute; the correct fix depends on the cause.

The window opens but won’t stay aligned

Loose or worn friction hinges can let the sash sag or catch the frame. Hinge condition and sash alignment should be checked before changing locking parts.

The window is stuck in the open position

A seized gearbox, damaged locking strip or misaligned locking points can prevent the handle returning to its normal position. Avoid repeatedly forcing the mechanism.

Can a broken UPVC window mechanism be repaired?

Often, yes. Depending on the fault and parts availability, a damaged handle, gearbox, espagnolette locking strip, keeps or friction hinges may be repaired, adjusted or replaced without fitting a whole new window. Some older or discontinued mechanisms may need a suitable alternative.

What should I do if my window will not close?

Do not force the handle or sash. Check for an obvious obstruction without dismantling the mechanism. If the window cannot be secured, keep the area safe and arrange an inspection. Photos of the handle, frame and locking edge can help identify the type of mechanism.
