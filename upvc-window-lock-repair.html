UPVC window locking problems

A window that will not open or secure properly can be caused by the handle, spindle, locking mechanism, keeps or alignment. The correct repair starts by establishing where the failure actually is.

Window jammed shut

A failed mechanism can leave the locking points engaged even though the handle no longer operates normally.

Handle turns or feels loose

A damaged handle, spindle or internal mechanism can prevent the lock from operating.

Window won't lock

Mechanism wear or alignment can prevent the locking points engaging correctly.

Repairing the existing window

Where the window and frame remain serviceable, repairing or replacing the faulty locking components can often restore normal operation without replacing the whole window.
