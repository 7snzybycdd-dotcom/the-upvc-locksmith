HELPFUL INFORMATION

Repair the fault, not automatically the whole door

Problems with a UPVC door do not automatically mean the door itself needs replacing. Depending on the fault and availability of suitable components, the issue may be within the gearbox, multipoint strip, cylinder, handle, keeps or door alignment.

ELY UPVC LOCKSMITH FAQ

Common questions

Do you repair UPVC door locks in Ely?

Yes. Ely is within our working area for UPVC and composite door and window lock problems.

Can a failed multipoint lock be repaired?

That depends on the mechanism and the failed component. The first step is identifying the lock and the cause of the fault.

My door only locks if I lift or push it. What could cause that?

Door alignment is one possibility. If the mechanism operates normally while the door is open but becomes stiff when closed, alignment and the keeps should be checked.

Do you only work on UPVC locks?

No. UPVC and composite door and window locks are our speciality, but general locksmith work is also undertaken.
