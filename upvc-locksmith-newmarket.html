SPECIALIST DIAGNOSIS

Find the cause before changing parts

Replacing parts without establishing why the lock has become difficult to operate can miss the real problem. For example, a door that has dropped can place extra pressure on a working multipoint mechanism, while an internal gearbox failure can leave a door unable to unlock even when the cylinder turns.

Do you cover Newmarket?

Can you replace a UPVC door gearbox?

Why is my UPVC door handle difficult to lift?

Possible causes include door alignment, pressure on the locking points or a fault within the mechanism. Testing the system helps distinguish between them.
