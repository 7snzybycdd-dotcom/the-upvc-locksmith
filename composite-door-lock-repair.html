Composite door locking faults

Composite doors commonly use multipoint locking systems. Problems may involve the central gearbox, locking strip, cylinder, handles, keeps or door alignment, so symptoms should be diagnosed before deciding what needs replacement.

Door won't lock

Difficulty lifting the handle or engaging the locking points may be mechanical or alignment related.

Failed gearbox

Internal gearbox faults can prevent the handle and key from operating the multipoint mechanism correctly.

Misaligned door

Excess pressure between the locking points and keeps can make an otherwise working mechanism difficult to operate.
